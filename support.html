Support

Contact

Email support@dtkfoundry.com with the tool's name and what happened. We reply within two business days. For an Apify actor you can also open an issue on the actor's page; issues are answered within one business day.

Refunds

If a paid plan doesn't work for you, email us within 14 days of the charge for a full refund.

Removing an add-on

In Docs, Sheets or Slides: Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons → the add-on's menu → Uninstall. To remove its access to your Google account as well, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.
