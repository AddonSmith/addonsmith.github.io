Small, focused add-ons for Google Docs, Sheets and Slides

Each add-on does one job well, works only on the document you have open, and sends nothing about your content anywhere else.

Find and Replace with Regex

For Docs, Sheets and Slides. Find and replace with plain text or regular expressions, preview every match before you change it, and run a batch of replacements in one go.

Statistics & Data Analysis for Sheets

Descriptive statistics, histograms, t-tests, ANOVA, correlation and regression, written to a new sheet with a plain-language note under each result.

Sheets to Calendar Sync

Turn rows in a sheet into Google Calendar events. Re-running updates events instead of duplicating them.

The add-ons are in development and will be listed on the Google Workspace Marketplace. Questions: see Support.
