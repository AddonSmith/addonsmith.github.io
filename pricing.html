Pricing

Every add-on is free to install and useful without paying. Pro unlocks extra features for the Google account you buy with. Prices are in US dollars; sales tax or VAT is added at checkout where it applies.

Data Analysis & Statistics for Sheets

Free

Pro: $24 a year

Everything in Free, plus chi-square tests, two-way ANOVA, Mann-Whitney and Wilcoxon, Tukey post-hoc, APA-style tables, charts with error bars, and saved analyses that re-run on new data. Also available monthly at $4.

Lifetime: $59 once

How Pro turns on

Payments are processed by Stripe through Link, our merchant of record, which also sends your receipt. Check out with the email of the Google account you use in Sheets; Pro turns on within a minute (in the add-on, choose Restore purchase if it doesn't). One purchase covers one Google account. The add-on checks your email with Stripe at most once a day; your documents are never sent. See the privacy policy.

Cancelling and refunds

Cancel a subscription any time at link.com or from your receipt; Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Not happy? Email us within 14 days of a charge for a full refund. Details: Refunds.
